# SEO Copilot – SEO Checker

Review the page you are viewing in Chrome with SEO Copilot. The extension checks common page elements, prioritizes issues, and suggests practical next steps.

SEO Copilot analyzes the page you choose and turns complex SEO checks into an easy-to-understand SEO score, prioritized issues, plain-language explanations, and actionable optimization recommendations.

No account required. No complicated setup.

## Privacy

The basic page scan runs locally in your browser, with no account required. If you request an AI suggestion, the extension sends the page context needed to generate that suggestion. You review and apply all changes yourself.

## Your complete SEO audit report

Every SEO scan provides:

- **0–100 SEO score** with an overall page health evaluation
- **Category analysis** for titles, meta descriptions, headings, images, content, and technical SEO
- **Issue prioritization** with critical, warning, and informational levels
- **Why it matters** — simple explanations of every SEO problem
- **How to fix it** — clear steps you can apply immediately

## What it checks

The report covers important page signals, including:

- Title tags and meta descriptions
- Heading structure (H1, H2, H3)
- Image alt text and file size
- URL structure and readability
- Canonical tags and duplicate content signals
- JSON-LD structured data
- Content quality and word count
- Technical SEO elements affecting website optimization

## Use it before and after updates

### Before publishing

Find missing titles, weak content, and structure issues before launching your webpage.

### After editing

Verify that your changes have not negatively affected your SEO performance.

### While improving a page

Review the score and issue list while fixing page issues.

### For client projects

Quickly review a page and identify areas that need improvement.

- **Fast page review** — check a webpage without uploads or waiting
- **Actionable recommendations** — get practical fixes, not just scores
- **Privacy-first approach** — webpage analysis happens locally in your browser
- **Learn SEO while optimizing** — understand every issue with simple explanations
- **Free to use** — review the available checks without feature limitations

## Start improving your SEO today

Click **Add to Chrome** to install SEO Copilot.

Then visit any webpage and click the extension icon to get a score, issue list, and recommendations within seconds.

No account. No setup. No complexity.

It is a focused way to review a page before or after an update, while keeping every website change under your control.
