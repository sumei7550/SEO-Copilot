# SEO Copilot

Audit the SEO of any webpage in seconds—directly in your browser.

SEO Copilot analyzes the page you choose and turns technical checks into a clear score, prioritized issues, impact explanations, and actionable fixes.

## What it checks

- Page title and meta description
- H1, H2, and H3 structure
- Image alternative text and large-image opportunities
- URL length and structure
- Canonical URL and JSON-LD structured data
- Content word count and visible-text ratio

## What you get

- A 0–100 SEO score and health grade
- Category scores for title, meta, headings, images, content, and technical SEO
- Critical, warning, and informational issue levels
- Plain-language explanations of why each issue matters
- Practical suggestions for fixing each issue

## Privacy by design

SEO Copilot analyzes only the active page you explicitly select. Processing happens locally in your browser. Page data is not stored, uploaded, sold, or shared.

No account is required. No analytics trackers. No remote code.
