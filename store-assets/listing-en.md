# SEO Copilot – SEO Checker

Analyze any webpage’s SEO quality in seconds. SEO Copilot is a powerful SEO tool that works as a website SEO checker and on-page SEO analyzer, helping you find optimization issues, understand their impact, and get clear solutions to improve your website.

SEO Copilot analyzes the page you choose and turns complex SEO checks into an easy-to-understand SEO score, prioritized issues, plain-language explanations, and actionable optimization recommendations.

No account required. No data upload. No complicated setup.

## Everything stays on your device

Unlike cloud-based SEO tools, SEO Copilot analyzes webpages directly in your browser.

- No data upload — analysis is processed locally in your browser
- No account required — start your SEO check instantly
- No tracking — no usage or analytics data is collected
- No remote processing — everything runs locally on your device

Perfect for analyzing client websites, pre-launch pages, or sensitive content.

## Built for website owners, not just SEO experts

SEO Copilot is perfect if you:

- Manage your own website or blog
- Create content and want to improve SEO performance
- Optimize webpages for better search visibility
- Build websites for clients and need quick SEO checks
- Want to learn SEO by fixing real optimization issues
- Need fast SEO analysis without complicated tools

## Your complete SEO audit report

Every SEO scan provides:

- **0–100 SEO score** with an overall page health evaluation
- **Category analysis** for titles, meta descriptions, headings, images, content, and technical SEO
- **Issue prioritization** with critical, warning, and informational levels
- **Why it matters** — simple explanations of every SEO problem
- **How to fix it** — clear steps you can apply immediately

## Advanced on-page SEO analysis

SEO Copilot checks 20+ important SEO factors, including:

- Title tags and meta descriptions
- Heading structure (H1, H2, H3)
- Image alt text and file size
- URL structure and readability
- Canonical tags and duplicate content signals
- JSON-LD structured data
- Content quality and word count
- Technical SEO elements affecting website optimization
